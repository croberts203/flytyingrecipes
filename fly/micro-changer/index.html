Micro Changer

Streamer (articulated) · advanced · Hooks #6–10 trailing hook · Freshwater · Pattern by Blane Chocklett (Game Changer platform)

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

A tiny Game Changer on micro shanks for trout, panfish and small bass; the same swimming action scaled down to minnow and fry size.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Baitfish

How to fish it

Fish it on a 5–6 weight with short strips; deadly when fish key on small baitfish.

Materials

  • Hook: Short streamer hook, #6–10
  • Shanks: Micro shanks, 6–15mm
  • Thread: White 70–100 denier
  • Tail: A few fine synthetic fibres
  • Body: Micro chenille or fine brush
  • Eyes: Small 3D eyes
  • Colour: Permanent markers
  • Glue: Brush-on superglue

Step by step

  1. Tie a tiny tail and a few turns of chenille on the hook.
  2. Link it to the first micro shank and wrap a slightly fuller section.
  3. Repeat up three or four shanks.
  4. Trim to a small minnow shape, colour it and add the eyes.
