Simple Midge Dry

Midge · beginner · Hooks #18–24 · Freshwater · Pattern by InTheRiffle

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown TroutBrook Trout

A tiny adult midge with a thread body, a short white wing for visibility and a few turns of grizzly hackle.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Midges

When: Winter

How to fish it

Fish it to sipping trout on flat winter water, on 6X or 7X tippet.

Materials

  • Hook: Dry fly hook, #18–24
  • Thread: Black 8/0
  • Body: Black thread, thin
  • Wing: White Antron or poly yarn, tied short
  • Hackle: Grizzly, two or three turns

Step by step

  1. Wrap a thin, even thread body to the bend and back.
  2. Tie in a short tuft of white yarn as a wing.
  3. Tie in a small grizzly hackle and wind two or three turns.
  4. Trim the wing short and whip finish.
