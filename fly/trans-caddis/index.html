Trans Caddis

Caddis pupa / emerger · intermediate · Hooks #10–14 wet or grub · Freshwater · Pattern by Davy Wotton (tied by Chris Reeves, Flydresser Summer 2016)

Rainbow TroutBrown Trout

A versatile caddis pupa and emerger: hare's ear body with ostrich herl and gold rib, a Swiss straw wing case, knotted pheasant tail legs, a tan CDC puff wing and a partridge hackle.

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Caddis (sedge) pupa swimming up to hatch, and the emerging adult

When: May to September, especially evenings; caddis hatch all season on many rivers

How to fish it

On the top dropper during a caddis hatch, draw it up and dibble it just under the surface for splashy takes. Fished deeper as a swimming pupa, pause it; takes often come a few seconds after it stops. Use a finer rib and more dubbing for the emerger, a heavier hook and less dubbing for the pupa.

Materials

  • Hook: Wet or grub hook, #10–14 (heavier for fishing deep)
  • Thread: Tan or brown
  • Ribs: Fine wire and gold tinsel
  • Body: Hare's ear dubbing
  • Ostrich: Ostrich herl
  • Wing case: Swiss straw or Raffine
  • Legs: Two pairs of knotted pheasant tail fibres
  • Wing: Light tan CDC oiler puff
  • Hackle: Partridge or hen

Step by step

  1. Tie in the wire and gold tinsel ribs and dub a hare's ear body.
  2. Wind ostrich herl over the body, then counter-wrap the gold tinsel to protect it.
  3. Shape a piece of Swiss straw and tie it on top as a wing case, keeping the head area clear.
  4. Tie in two pairs of knotted pheasant tail legs, one pair each side.
  5. Tie in a tan CDC puff as a wing, trimming the thick stem cleanly so it doesn't leave a lump.
  6. Wind two turns of partridge or hen, folded back. Form a neat head and whip finish.
