Supersized Golden Olive Bumble

Loch wet fly (supersized) · intermediate · Hooks #4–10 salmon · Freshwater · Pattern by Chris Reeves (Flydresser Autumn 2016)

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Brown TroutAtlantic Salmon

Kingsmill Moore's Golden Olive Bumble scaled up for salmon: topping tail, golden olive seal's fur body, gold rib, two palmered golden olive hackles and a collar of blue guinea fowl over golden pheasant yellow rump.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

How to fish it

Supersized loch flies for salmon in a spate-river-fed Scottish loch. Fish them bushy and on the move to create a disturbance; tied small, they're the classic trout patterns.

Materials

  • Hook: Salmon hook (e.g. Partridge Patriot), #4–10
  • Thread: Black 8/0
  • Tail: Golden pheasant topping
  • Rib: Oval gold tinsel
  • Body: Golden olive seal's fur
  • Body hackle: Two golden olive cock hackles
  • Collar: Golden pheasant yellow rump feather, then blue guinea fowl

Step by step

  1. Tie in a golden pheasant topping tail and the oval gold rib.
  2. Dub a golden olive seal's fur body.
  3. Palmer two golden olive hackles from the front back to the tail and lock them with the rib, winding it forward through them.
  4. Wind a golden pheasant yellow rump feather as a collar, then blue guinea fowl in front of it.
  5. Form the head and whip finish.
