LaFontaine Deep Pupa

Nymph · beginner · Hooks #12–16 · Freshwater · Pattern by Gary LaFontaine

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown TroutBrook Trout

Gary LaFontaine's caddis pupa with an Antron shuck, a dubbed body, speckled legs and a fur or ostrich head.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Caddis pupae

When: Summer

How to fish it

Fish it deep and let it rise.

Materials

  • Hook: 2X-long nymph hook, #12–16
  • Weight: Non-toxic wire
  • Thread: Brown or tan
  • Shuck: Tan or brown Antron
  • Body: Tan, brown or green dubbing
  • Legs: Mallard, wood duck or partridge
  • Head: Brown fur or ostrich

Step by step

  1. Wrap non-toxic wire on the shank.
  2. Tie in a short Antron shuck at the bend.
  3. Dub the body and tie in speckled legs on the sides.
  4. Dub a fur or ostrich head.
