Supersized Peter Ross

Loch wet fly (supersized) · intermediate · Hooks #4–10 salmon · Freshwater · Pattern by Chris Reeves (Flydresser Autumn 2016)

Brown TroutAtlantic Salmon

The Fly Dressers' Guild emblem fly, scaled up: topping tail, silver rear body and scarlet seal's fur front, oval silver rib, black hen collar and (because teal doesn't come that long) a grey squirrel wing.

How to fish it

Supersized loch flies for salmon in a spate-river-fed Scottish loch. Fish them bushy and on the move to create a disturbance; tied small, they're the classic trout patterns.

Materials

  • Hook: Salmon hook (e.g. Partridge Patriot), #4–10
  • Thread: Black 8/0
  • Tail: Golden pheasant topping
  • Rib: Oval silver tinsel
  • Rear body: Silver tinsel or Mylar
  • Front body: Scarlet seal's fur
  • Wing: Natural grey squirrel (teal on small sizes)
  • Collar: Black hen hackle

Step by step

  1. Tie in a topping tail and the oval silver rib.
  2. Wind the rear half of the body in flat silver tinsel, then dub the front half in scarlet seal's fur. Rib the whole body.
  3. Take a wrap of thread around a bunch of grey squirrel before setting it on top; it stops the hair rolling down the side. Tie it in as the wing.
  4. Trim the butts short and seal them with a drop of varnish.
  5. Add a black hen beard or collar, form a neat head and whip finish.
