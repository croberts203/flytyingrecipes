Foam-Backed Fry

Fry (floating) · beginner · Hooks #10 nymph · Freshwater · Pattern by Phil Bilbrough (Flydresser Spring 2019)

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown Trout

A simple, very effective floating fry: a silver fritz body under a white foam back with a V-notched tail.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Perch or roach fry (pinfry) near the surface

When: Mid-summer into autumn (June onward) when shoals of perch and roach fry gather in the margins

How to fish it

Fish it alone or on the point when trout are chasing pinfry near the top. It's quick to tie and easy to replace.

Materials

  • Hook: Nymph hook, #10
  • Thread: White 8/0
  • Body: 6mm silver fritz
  • Back: Strip of white ethafoam, V-notched at the tail

Step by step

  1. Start the thread at the bend and leave a few inches of the tag end hanging there; you'll use it to tie down the foam.
  2. Tie in the foam strip at the bend, then the silver fritz, and wind the fritz forward to the eye.
  3. Pull the foam over the back and tie it down at the head.
  4. Use the hanging thread tag to bind the foam down at the bend, whip finish it there and cut a V in the foam tail.
  5. Whip finish at the head and varnish both ends.
