Hoh Bo Spey

Steelhead spey · advanced · Hooks Shank + #2 intruder · Freshwater · Pattern by Tim Flagler (Fly Tyer)

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow Trout

A shank-style steelhead spey fly: pink butt, purple Ice Dub body over a pink guinea rib support, black marabou and fuchsia flash collar, with a pink Amherst front collar.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

How to fish it

Swing it for winter steelhead on sink-tips.

Materials

  • Shank: 25mm articulated shank
  • Connection: 30 lb braided line
  • Hook: Intruder hook, #2
  • Thread: Black 140 denier
  • Butt: Pink UV Ice Dub
  • Rib support: Pink-dyed guinea hen
  • Body: Purple UV Ice Dub
  • Collar: Black spey marabou with fuchsia flash
  • Front collar: Pink Lady Amherst fibres

Step by step

  1. Attach the hook to the shank with a braided loop.
  2. Dub a pink butt and wind a pink guinea feather as a support collar.
  3. Dub a purple body.
  4. Wind black marabou with fuchsia flash, then a pink Amherst collar.
  5. Whip finish.
