Ivens' March Brown

Wet fly / nymph · beginner · Hooks #12–15 wet · Freshwater · Pattern by Tom Ivens (Adrian Freer, Flydresser Summer 2021)

Rainbow TroutBrown Trout

Tom Ivens' river variation of the Gold Ribbed Hare's Ear: two partridge fibre tails, a rough hare's ear body ribbed gold, a dark partridge collar and a bold peacock herl head.

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: March brown nymph or emerger (Rhithrogena germanica); also a general olive nymph

When: March brown hatches March–May around midday on rivers; general use all season

How to fish it

A general-purpose river wet. A little roughness adds to its appeal; it suggests olive nymphs, caddis, shrimp or emergers.

Materials

  • Hook: Wet fly hook, #12–15
  • Thread: Black
  • Tail: Two speckled partridge fibres
  • Body: Hare's ear (or chopped rabbit or hare guard hair)
  • Rib: Oval gold tinsel
  • Hackle: Dark partridge collar
  • Head: Peacock herl, twisted

Step by step

  1. Lay a thread base and tie in two speckled partridge fibres as tails, then the gold rib.
  2. Dub a rough hare's ear body and rib it with the gold.
  3. Wind a dark partridge hackle as a collar.
  4. Twist a couple of peacock herls and wind a bold head in front of the hackle. Whip finish.
