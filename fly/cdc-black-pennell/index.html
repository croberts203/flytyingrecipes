CDC Black Pennell

Wet fly · intermediate · Hooks #8–14 heavy wet · Freshwater · Pattern by Lawrence Finney (Flydresser Spring 2019)

Rainbow TroutBrown Trout

The Black Pennell with two black CDC feathers in place of the hen hackle. The CDC holds air bubbles at first, then gives the fly lots of movement once it's wet.

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Hatching midge (buzzer) at the surface

When: April–September, especially evening buzzer hatches

How to fish it

Fish it as you would a normal Black Pennell, on the top dropper in a wave.

Materials

  • Hook: Heavy wet fly hook, #8–14
  • Thread: Claret 8/0
  • Tail: Golden pheasant tippet fibres
  • Tag: Red thread
  • Rib: Oval silver tinsel
  • Body: Black seal's fur
  • Hackle: Two black CDC feathers

Step by step

  1. Lay a thread base and make a small red thread tag at the rear.
  2. Tie in a few golden pheasant tippet fibres for the tail, then the oval silver rib.
  3. Dub a slim body of black seal's fur and rib it with open turns of tinsel.
  4. Wind two black CDC feathers as a hackle (if the stems are thick, put the fibres in a dubbing loop and wind that instead).
  5. Form a small head and whip finish.
