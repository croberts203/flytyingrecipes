Czech Nymph

Nymph · intermediate · Hooks #8–14 curved · Freshwater · Pattern by Czech competition anglers

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown TroutBrook Trout

A heavy, curved caddis-larva nymph: weighted body, dubbed segments, a shellback and a dark thorax.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Caddis larvae

When: All year

How to fish it

Fish it short-line in fast water.

Materials

  • Hook: Curved grub hook, #8–14
  • Thread: Brown 8/0
  • Weight: Lead-free wire or flat lead
  • Shellback: Scud back or plastic strip
  • Rib: Mono or wire
  • Body: Cream and olive dubbing
  • Thorax: Dark dubbing

Step by step

  1. Weight the hook heavily.
  2. Tie in the shellback and rib.
  3. Dub the body and thorax.
  4. Pull the shellback over, rib it and whip finish.
