Dirty Hippy

Streamer · intermediate · Hooks #4 · Freshwater · Pattern by InTheRiffle

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Largemouth BassRainbow TroutBrown TroutNorthern PikeSmallmouth Bass

A copper cone streamer with fox fur belly, a tan marabou wing, an arctic fox collar, a UV Ice Dub face and holographic eyes.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Baitfish

When: All year

How to fish it

Strip and swing it for trout and smallmouth.

Materials

  • Hook: TMC 5263, #4
  • Cone: Large copper brass cone
  • Weight: 0.025 lead wire
  • Thread: Tan 3/0 monocord
  • Flash: Gold, copper and pearl Flashabou
  • Belly: Cream temple dog or arctic fox
  • Wing: Tan marabou
  • Collar: Bonefish tan arctic fox
  • Face: UV tan Ice Dub
  • Eyes: 3/16 gold holographic eyes

Step by step

  1. Add the cone and lead.
  2. Tie in flash and a fox belly.
  3. Tie a marabou wing and a fox collar.
  4. Dub the face and glue on eyes.
