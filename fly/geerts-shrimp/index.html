Geert's Shrimp

Salmon shrimp · advanced · Hooks #2 (works larger or smaller, singles, doubles or trebles) · Freshwater · Pattern by Geert Werbrouck, Belgium (Flydresser Autumn 2021)

Atlantic Salmon

A two-part orange salmon shrimp with wood duck and golden pheasant feelers, a four-strand twisted gold and copper rib, yellow-dyed badger hackles and an orange collar.

How to fish it

A bright shrimp for salmon in coloured or falling water. If you fish a Turle knot, leave room at the eye.

Materials

  • Hook: Salmon single, heavy or standard iron, #2
  • Thread: White 8/0, then red for the head
  • Tag: Orange-hued copper wire
  • Feelers: Wood duck feather and a golden pheasant tippet
  • Rib: Two fine oval gold tinsels and two fine orange copper wires twisted into a rope
  • Body: Orange fur (raccoon underfur or seal's fur), two sections
  • Centre and front hackles: Well-marked badger cock, dyed yellow
  • Collar: Orange cock hackle

Step by step

  1. Cover the hook point with a bit of clear tubing to save your fingers. Wind a copper wire tag to just above the point.
  2. In front of the tag, wind a wood duck feather, folding the fibres back as feelers. Cut a V in a short golden pheasant tippet and tie it on top so it frames the tail. Keep both waste ends.
  3. Bind the waste ends along the shank into a smooth underbody tapering toward the eye, stopping well short of it.
  4. Tie in the twisted four-strand rib, dub orange fur over the rear half and make three even turns of rib. Tie off.
  5. Fold a yellow badger hackle and wind two turns, sweeping the fibres back.
  6. Dub the front half, rib it the same way and wind two turns of orange hackle as a collar.
  7. Switch to red thread, wind two turns of a second yellow badger hackle in front, then form a neat head, whip finish and varnish.
