Pike Bunny Fly

Pike streamer · beginner · Hooks #2/0–4/0 pike · Freshwater · Pattern by Classic pike pattern

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Largemouth BassNorthern Pike

A long rabbit strip with flash and a wound rabbit collar on a strong hook. Simple, cheap and deadly.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

How to fish it

Strip it with long pauses; always use a bite tippet.

Materials

  • Hook: Strong pike hook, #2/0–4/0
  • Thread: Strong 3/0
  • Tail: Rabbit zonker strip, 4–6 inches
  • Flash: Flashabou
  • Collar: Rabbit strip, wound
  • Eyes: Stick-on or dumbbell eyes

Step by step

  1. Tie in a long zonker strip tail with flash.
  2. Wind a rabbit collar forward.
  3. Add eyes and whip finish.
