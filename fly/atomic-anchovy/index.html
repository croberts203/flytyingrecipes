Atomic Anchovy

Baitfish · intermediate · Hooks #2 – 2/0 saltwater · Saltwater · Pattern by Flydresser, Summer 2010

Illustration by Fly Bench showing the general look of the pattern.

Jack CrevalleLadyfishSpanish MackerelBluefish

A saltwater anchovy with a pearl mylar body, white hair with blue and black flash, grizzly cheeks and an extended mylar hood over a cone head, finished with epoxy eyes.

How to fish it

Fast strips around bait balls for bluefish, Spanish mackerel and jacks. The cone and epoxy head give it a darting, jiggy action.

Materials

  • Hook: Saltwater hook, #2–2/0
  • Thread: White, flat-waxed nylon
  • Head: Cone head on a short piece of flared tube liner
  • Body: Pearl mylar tube, core removed
  • Wing: White or cream yak-hair substitute
  • Flash: Blue and black Krystal Flash
  • Cheeks: Two well-marked grizzly hackles
  • Hood: Large pearl mylar tube, end sealed with a flame
  • Eyes: Stick-on or epoxy eyes
  • Finish: Epoxy, black marker, superglue

Step by step

  1. Lay a thread base. Slide a short piece of tube liner, flared at the eye end with a flame, onto the shank, push the cone over it and lock it with a drop of superglue.
  2. Behind the cone, tie in a length of cored-out pearl mylar tube balloon-style for the body, with a touch of superglue at the rear tie-off.
  3. Directly behind the cone, tie in fine white hair above and below the shank, then blue Krystal Flash and a little black Krystal Flash on top.
  4. Tie a grizzly hackle on each side as a cheek, running the length of the body. Secure and glue the butts.
  5. Cut a piece of wide pearl mylar tube that slips over the cone. Carefully seal the cut end with a flame so the strands fuse (mask the rest so it doesn't catch).
  6. Cut it to about half the body length, tie it on in front of the cone with the sealed end facing forward, then roll it back over the cone to form an extended hood.
  7. Colour the top of the hood with a black marker, add an eye on each side and coat the whole head with epoxy, turning it while it cures.
