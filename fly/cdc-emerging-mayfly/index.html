CDC Emerging Mayfly

Mayfly (emerger) · intermediate · Hooks #10 dry · Freshwater · Pattern by Lawrence Finney (Flydresser Spring 2019)

Rainbow TroutBrown Trout

An emerging mayfly with Coq de Leon tails, an olive seal's fur body, a pale olive CDC wing under a pheasant tail wing case, and a darker olive CDC hackle.

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Mayfly (Ephemera danica) emerger

When: Late May to mid-June

How to fish it

Fish it in the surface film during the mayfly hatch, letting the CDC wing sit up.

Materials

  • Hook: Standard dry fly hook, #10
  • Thread: Red 8/0
  • Tail: Coq de Leon fibres
  • Rib: Fine silver wire
  • Body: Olive green seal's fur
  • Wing: Light olive or yellow CDC feather
  • Wing case: Cock pheasant tail fibres
  • Hackle: Two darker olive CDC feathers

Step by step

  1. Tie in a few Coq de Leon fibres as the tail and the silver wire rib.
  2. Dub an olive seal's fur body and rib it.
  3. Tie in a bunch of pheasant tail fibres for the wing case, then a light olive CDC feather as the wing.
  4. Pull the pheasant tail forward over the base of the CDC wing and tie it down.
  5. Wind two darker olive CDC feathers as a soft wet-fly hackle, form the head and whip finish.
