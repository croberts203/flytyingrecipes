Hydro Fly

Salmon / sea trout hairwing · intermediate · Hooks #2 (and smaller) · Freshwater · Pattern by Jimmy Younger, Dumfries (Flydresser Spring 2011)

Brown TroutAtlantic Salmon

A Scottish hairwing in an unusual colour mix: blue tinsel body ribbed silver, gold tag, topping tail, red wool butt, orange and guinea fowl hackles, and grey squirrel veiled with blue squirrel.

How to fish it

A salmon and sea trout fly. Add jungle cock cheeks for brighter conditions.

Materials

  • Hook: Salmon hook (e.g. Partridge Bartleet CS10/2), #2
  • Thread: Black
  • Tag: Fine oval gold tinsel
  • Tail: Small golden pheasant topping
  • Butt: Red wool
  • Rib: Medium oval silver tinsel
  • Body: Blue flat tinsel
  • Hackles: Soft orange cock, then guinea fowl
  • Wing: Grey squirrel under blue-dyed grey squirrel
  • Cheeks: Jungle cock (optional)

Step by step

  1. Wind a short tag of fine oval gold tinsel and tie in a small golden pheasant topping as the tail.
  2. Make a small butt of red wool.
  3. Tie in the oval silver rib under the shank at the butt, and the blue tinsel a few millimetres behind the eye. Wind the tinsel back to the butt and forward again, then secure it.
  4. Rib forward and tie off under the shank.
  5. Wind two or three turns of a soft orange hackle, then two turns of guinea fowl. Wet your fingers and stroke the hackles down under the shank.
  6. Tie in a small bunch of grey squirrel no longer than the bend, with a slightly smaller bunch of blue squirrel over it to veil the top and sides.
  7. Add jungle cock cheeks if you like, form the head and varnish twice.
