Chubby Muffin

Streamer · intermediate · Hooks #2–10 · Freshwater · Pattern by InTheRiffle

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Largemouth BassRainbow TroutBrown TroutNorthern PikeSmallmouth Bass

A sculpin streamer with pseudo eyes, a grizzly rabbit tail, a sculpin SLF body, red schlappen gills, hen back fins and a craft fur head.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Sculpins

When: All year

How to fish it

Bounce it along the bottom in riffles and runs.

Materials

  • Hook: Tiemco 8089, #2–10
  • Thread: Olive 140 denier
  • Eyes: Large pseudo eyes
  • Tail: Grizzly rabbit strip
  • Flash: Gold holographic Flashabou
  • Body: Brown sculpin SLF dubbing
  • Gills: Red schlappen
  • Fins: Natural India hen back
  • Head: Yellow craft fur

Step by step

  1. Tie on the eyes and a rabbit strip tail with flash.
  2. Dub a full SLF body.
  3. Add red schlappen gills and hen back fins on each side.
  4. Build a craft fur head around the eyes and trim it broad.
