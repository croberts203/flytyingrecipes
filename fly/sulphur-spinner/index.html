Sulphur Spinner

Dry fly · intermediate · Hooks #14 · Freshwater · Pattern by Tim Cammisa

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown TroutBrook Trout

A sulphur spinner with microfibbet tails, a turkey biot body, snowshoe rabbit spent wings, a UV flash wing case and a sulphur dubbing thorax.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Sulphur spinners

When: Late spring to summer

How to fish it

Fish it at dusk when sulphur spinners fall.

Materials

  • Hook: Daiichi 1110, #14
  • Thread: Tan 8/0
  • Tail: Light blue dun microfibbets
  • Body: Sulphur yellow turkey biot
  • Wing: Snowshoe rabbit foot, spent
  • Wing case: UV Flashabou
  • Thorax: Sulphur dubbing

Step by step

  1. Tie in split microfibbet tails.
  2. Wrap a biot body.
  3. Tie in spent snowshoe wings and the flash wing case.
  4. Dub the thorax and pull the flash over.
