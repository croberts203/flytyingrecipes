Red Fox Squirrel Nymph

Nymph · beginner · Hooks #8–16 2XL · Freshwater · Pattern by Dave Whitlock

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown TroutBrook Trout

A buggy fox squirrel nymph: a squirrel hair tail, a lighter belly-fur abdomen ribbed with gold and a darker back-fur thorax with partridge legs.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Mayfly and stonefly nymphs

When: All season

How to fish it

An all-round searching nymph that suggests many mayflies and stoneflies.

Materials

  • Hook: 2X-long nymph hook, #8–16
  • Thread: Black 8/0
  • Weight: Lead-free wire
  • Tail: Fox squirrel back hair
  • Rib: Oval gold tinsel
  • Abdomen: Fox squirrel belly dubbing
  • Thorax: Fox squirrel back dubbing
  • Legs: Partridge

Step by step

  1. Weight the shank and tie in the tail and rib.
  2. Dub the abdomen and rib it.
  3. Dub a rough thorax.
  4. Wind a turn of partridge and whip finish.
