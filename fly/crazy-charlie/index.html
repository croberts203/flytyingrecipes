Crazy Charlie (Pompano)

Shrimp · beginner · Hooks #6 – #2 · Saltwater

Photo: Mike Cline, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons. Example of the pattern, not tied by Fly Bench.

RedfishBlack DrumFlorida PompanoWhiting

A slim, bead-chain-eyed shrimp/sand-flea imitation that rides point up. Small, sparse and quick to tie.

How to fish it

Pink, chartreuse or tan in the surf for pompano and whiting, using a sinking line in the trough. Short, quick strips.

Materials

  • Hook: Saltwater hook, #4–#2
  • Thread: Fine thread, pink or chartreuse
  • Eyes: Silver bead-chain eyes
  • Body: Pearl flat tinsel, overwrapped with clear vinyl rib or UV resin
  • Wing: Calf tail or craft fur, sparse, with 2–4 strands of flash

Step by step

  1. Tie in the bead-chain eyes on top of the shank just behind the eye.
  2. Tie in the tinsel and clear rib at the bend, wrap the tinsel forward to the eyes, then rib over it.
  3. Flip the hook point up.
  4. Tie in a sparse wing of calf tail with a few strands of flash, extending just past the bend.
  5. Build a small head, whip finish and cement.
