Three Dollar Dip

Nymph · beginner · Hooks #14–20 scud · Freshwater · Pattern by Tailwater classic

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown TroutBrook Trout

A simple brown thread pupa with a gold rib, a gold bead and a little bleached elk hair wing bud.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Midge pupae and emerging mayflies

When: All year

How to fish it

Fish it small under an indicator on tailwaters during midge and Baetis activity.

Materials

  • Hook: Scud or pupa hook, #14–20
  • Bead: Gold bead
  • Thread: Brown 6/0 (forms the body)
  • Rib: Fine gold wire
  • Wing buds: Bleached elk hair

Step by step

  1. Add the bead, tie in the wire and wrap a slim brown thread body.
  2. Rib it in even turns.
  3. Tie in a few bleached elk hairs on top behind the bead, trim them short as wing buds.
  4. Whip finish.
