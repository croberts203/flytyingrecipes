Thunder & Lightning (Spey variant)

Salmon fly (Spey) · advanced · Hooks #4 salmon · Freshwater · Pattern by Anders Ståhl, Sweden (Fly Dressers' Guild Pattern Library)

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown TroutAtlantic Salmon

A Spey-style fishing version of the classic Thunder and Lightning: gold and orange tags, black Antron body, orange palmered Spey hackle, blue shoulder and guinea fowl, and humped bronze mallard wings. Tougher and cheaper than the full dressing.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

How to fish it

A must in several sizes. Anders has caught salmon, coho, sea trout, rainbows, browns, perch, pike and grayling on it. Leave room at the eye for a Turle knot.

Materials

  • Hook: Salmon hook (e.g. TMC golden salmon), #4
  • Thread: Black 8/0
  • Tags: Oval gold tinsel #14, then orange or red Antron (halved)
  • Rib: Oval gold tinsel #14
  • Body: Black Antron yarn, halved
  • Body hackle: Heron, pheasant or schlappen dyed orange
  • Shoulder hackle: Heron, pheasant or schlappen dyed blue, a little longer
  • Collar: Blue guinea fowl
  • Wing: Bronze mallard, four slips

Step by step

  1. Above the hook point, wind three turns of oval gold tinsel, then a second tag of a few turns of orange Antron.
  2. Tie in the gold rib and take the thread to the eye.
  3. Prepare the orange body hackle: fibres as long as the shank, doubled (strip one side of a schlappen so it isn't bulky).
  4. Tie in black Antron a few mm behind the eye and wind it back to the tag in diagonal touching turns. Come forward about 3mm and use it to trap the hackle tip, then wind an even, tight body to a few mm short of the eye.
  5. Rib forward in five open turns, then wind the body hackle forward in five turns, each tucked just behind a tinsel turn. Tie off.
  6. Wind two or three turns of the blue shoulder hackle, then three or four turns of doubled blue guinea fowl.
  7. Cut four bronze mallard slips (two left, two right), hump each to follow the shank, and tie two stacked slips on each side.
  8. Trim, shape a neat head, put a drop of superglue on it and varnish.
