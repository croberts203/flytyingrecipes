Morrish Hopper

Terrestrial · intermediate · Hooks #6–12 2XL · Freshwater · Pattern by Mark Morrish

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown TroutBrook Trout

A modern foam hopper: a layered foam body cut to shape, rubber legs and an elk or poly wing.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Grasshoppers

When: July–September

How to fish it

Bombs it into the banks and it floats all day; a great dropper carrier.

Materials

  • Hook: 2X-long dry hook, #6–12
  • Thread: Tan 3/0
  • Body: Layered 2mm foam, cut to shape
  • Wing: Elk hair or poly
  • Legs: Rubber legs

Step by step

  1. Cut and glue a two-colour foam body.
  2. Tie it down at the rear and front segments.
  3. Add the wing and legs at the front segment.
  4. Whip finish under the foam.
