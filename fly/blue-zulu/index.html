Blue Zulu

Wet fly · beginner · Hooks #10–14 · Freshwater · Pattern by Traditional loch fly

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown TroutBrook Trout

A loch-style palmered wet fly with a red wool tail, black seal's fur body, black body hackle and a teal-blue front hackle.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Attractor

When: Spring to fall

How to fish it

Fish it on the top dropper in a wave on lakes.

Materials

  • Hook: Wet fly hook, #10–14
  • Thread: Black 8/0
  • Tail: Red wool
  • Rib: Flat silver tinsel
  • Body: Black seal's fur or substitute
  • Body hackle: Black hen or cock
  • Front hackle: Teal blue hen or cock

Step by step

  1. Tie in the red wool tail.
  2. Dub a black body and palmer the body hackle.
  3. Rib the body with silver tinsel.
  4. Wind the blue front hackle.
