Grafham Shrimp

Shrimp (freshwater) · beginner · Hooks #12 heavy grub · Freshwater · Pattern by Chris Reeves (Flydresser Summer 2022)

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown Trout

A simple freshwater shrimp: grey partridge tail and whisks, a spiky hare's ear body segmented with gold tinsel, a clear polythene shellback and a mono over-rib. Add a dark bead or lead for weight.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Freshwater shrimp (Gammarus)

When: All year; especially important in colder months and in clear, weedy or rocky water

How to fish it

Made for Grafham. Best as a dropper behind a weighted nymph, fished tight to stones and dam walls; shrimp feeders are often in water just inches deep.

Materials

  • Hook: Heavy grub hook (e.g. Partridge K5AY), #12
  • Thread: Red 8/0
  • Tail and whisks: Grey partridge fibres
  • Over-rib: Clear 6 lb mono
  • Shellback: 3mm strip of clear polythene
  • Under-rib: Fine gold tinsel
  • Body: Hare's ear or other spiky dubbing
  • Weight: Black or dark red bead, or lead wire (optional)

Step by step

  1. Lay thread halfway down the bend and tie in a bunch of grey partridge fibres for the tail.
  2. Tie in the mono over-rib, then the polythene shellback and the gold under-rib at the same point.
  3. Take the thread forward, tie in a few more partridge fibres as head whisks, and return to the rear.
  4. Dub a fairly heavy body and wind the gold rib in 8–9 tight open turns for segments.
  5. Pull the shellback over and secure it at the head.
  6. Wind the mono over the back, following the tinsel turns. Trim only when the shellback sits evenly, then whip finish and brush out the body for legs.
