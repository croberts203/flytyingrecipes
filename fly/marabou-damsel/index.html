Marabou Damsel

Nymph (damsel) · beginner · Hooks #10 long shank · Freshwater · Pattern by Flydresser, Spring 2004

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown Trout

A damsel made almost entirely of marabou: a long olive tail, a twisted-marabou body ribbed silver, a brown marabou back and a teased-out olive thorax behind a gold bead.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Damselfly nymph (Zygoptera), swimming

When: Late May through August, on warm sunny days

How to fish it

Fish it slowly around weed beds and margins. Make the thorax about half the length of the body.

Materials

  • Hook: Long shank, #10
  • Bead: Gold bead
  • Thread: Brown or black
  • Tail and body: Olive marabou
  • Back: Brown marabou
  • Rib: Narrow silver tinsel

Step by step

  1. Slide on the bead, secure it with thread and a little varnish, and run the thread to opposite the barb.
  2. Tie in a bunch of olive marabou leaving about two inches as tail, and a 10cm length of silver tinsel.
  3. Take the thread halfway to the bead. Twist the marabou into a tight rope, wrap it to the thread and tie off. Rib with four or five open turns.
  4. Tie in a bunch of brown marabou on top and a smaller bunch of olive marabou.
  5. Twist the olive into a rope and wrap a fatter thorax up to the bead. Pull the brown marabou over the top and tie it off at the bead.
  6. Whip finish, varnish, tease out the thorax with Velcro and pinch the tail to length.
