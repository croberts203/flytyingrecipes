BugPole

Nymph (bug) · beginner · Hooks #14 · Freshwater · Pattern by Flydresser, Winter 2007

Rainbow TroutBrown Trout

A small weighted shrimp-style bug with a pearl shellback, a twisted copper rib and a squirrel or hare body. Built for grayling and nymphing chalkstream browns.

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Damsel nymph or small leech

When: May–September on stillwaters

How to fish it

Spot the fish, cast upstream and let it dead-drift. Any movement of the leader, or a fish shifting near the fly, means lift.

Materials

  • Hook: Grub or wet hook, #14
  • Thread: Brown or olive
  • Weight: 0.37mm lead wire, 8–9 turns
  • Shellback: Pearlescent sheet, 4mm strip
  • Rib: Fine copper wire twisted into a rope
  • Body: Dubbed squirrel or hare (or brown Microbrite)
  • Coating: Hard varnish over the back

Step by step

  1. Tie in a short length of lead wire about 1mm behind the eye and bind down 8–9 turns.
  2. Cut a 4mm strip of pearl sheet and tie it in at the tail.
  3. A millimetre or two forward of the tail (so it can't slip off), tie in the twisted copper rib.
  4. Dub hare or squirrel and make two turns behind the rib, then carry the body forward to the head.
  5. Stretch the pearl strip over the back, tie it down and trim. Bring the rib forward and make two turns at the head.
  6. Whip finish and coat the shellback with a heavy varnish.
