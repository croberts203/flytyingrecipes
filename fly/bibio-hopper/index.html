Bibio Hopper

Hopper (dry or wet) · beginner · Hooks #12 1.5X long · Freshwater · Pattern by Chris Reeves (Flydresser)

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown Trout

A stillwater hopper with a carrot-shaped seal's fur body in black, red and black, a stretched pearl rib, knotted pheasant tail legs and a black hackle. Also tie it in claret, black or fiery brown.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Heather fly (Bibio pomonae) and other black terrestrials blown onto the water

When: Heather fly: August–September; a general hopper all summer

How to fish it

Tie with a stiff cock hackle to fish dry, or a soft hen hackle to fish it wet or greased in the film. A summer favourite when trout look up.

Materials

  • Hook: Medium-wire 1.5X long dry hook, #12
  • Thread: Black 8/0
  • Rib: Pearl Mylar tinsel, stretched
  • Body: Seal's fur: black, red, black in equal parts
  • Legs: Six knotted pheasant tail fibres
  • Hackle: Black cock (dry) or hen (wet)

Step by step

  1. Lay a thread base and tie in the pearl Mylar. Stretch it until it turns pearly blue (if yours snaps instead, use a stretchable Mylar).
  2. Dub a carrot-shaped body that thickens toward the eye: black at the rear, red in the middle, black at the front.
  3. Rib tightly with the Mylar, tie it off securely and fluff the fur out with a Velcro brush.
  4. Tie six knotted pheasant tail legs on the underside so they reach just past the bend.
  5. Tie in the hackle and make about six wraps. Form a neat head, whip finish and varnish.
