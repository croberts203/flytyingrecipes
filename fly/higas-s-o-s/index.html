Higa's S.O.S.

Nymph · beginner · Hooks #16–20 · Freshwater · Pattern by Spencer Higa

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown TroutBrook Trout

Spencer Higa's small black nymph with a nickel bead, a silver rib, a red tinsel wing case, a possum thorax and Krystal Flash legs.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Midges, small nymphs

When: All year

How to fish it

Fish it as a dropper in tailwaters.

Materials

  • Hook: 2X-short emerger hook, #16–20
  • Bead: Nickel, 5/64 inch
  • Thread: Black 70 denier
  • Rib: Small silver Ultra Wire
  • Tail: Black pheasant tail
  • Wing case: Red holo tinsel or red floss
  • Thorax: Black Australian possum
  • Legs: Two strands of black Krystal Flash

Step by step

  1. Slide on the bead and tie in a short tail and the rib.
  2. Build a thread abdomen and rib it.
  3. Tie in the wing case and legs and dub the thorax.
  4. Pull the wing case over and finish behind the bead.
