Sucker Spawn

Egg · beginner · Hooks #12–16 short · Freshwater · Pattern by Pennsylvania classic

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown TroutBrook Trout

A cluster of loose yarn loops that looks like a clump of sucker or trout eggs.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Sucker and trout eggs

When: Spring

How to fish it

Dead-drift it in spring when suckers are spawning.

Materials

  • Hook: Short egg or scud hook, #12–16
  • Thread: Orange or white 6/0
  • Body: Egg yarn, wrapped in loops
  • Weight: Optional fine wire

Step by step

  1. Tie in a strand of yarn.
  2. Wrap it in small loops along the shank.
  3. Tie off and whip finish.
