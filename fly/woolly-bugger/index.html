Woolly Bugger

Streamer · beginner · Hooks #12 – #4 · Freshwater

Photo: Mike Cline, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons. Example of the pattern, not tied by Fly Bench.

Largemouth BassBluegillRedbreast SunfishBlack CrappieChannel Catfish

Marabou tail, chenille body, palmered hackle. Looks like a leech, minnow, crawfish or dragonfly nymph — whatever the fish want it to be.

How to fish it

Black or olive for bass and bream, white for crappie. Strip slowly near cover, or dead-drift in creeks for redbreast.

Materials

  • Hook: Streamer hook, 3XL, #6–#10
  • Thread: 6/0, black or olive
  • Head: Bead or cone (optional)
  • Tail: Marabou plus 2–4 strands of flash
  • Body: Medium chenille
  • Hackle: Saddle hackle, palmered
  • Rib: Fine wire

Step by step

  1. Slide on a bead if using one and start the thread behind it.
  2. Tie in a marabou tail about one shank length long, with a few strands of flash.
  3. Tie in the wire, the hackle by its tip, and the chenille at the bend.
  4. Wrap the chenille forward to the head and tie off.
  5. Palmer the hackle forward in open spirals and tie off.
  6. Counter-wrap the wire forward through the hackle to protect it, tie off and whip finish.
