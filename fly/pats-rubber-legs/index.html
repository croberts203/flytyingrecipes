Pat's Rubber Legs

Nymph (stonefly) · beginner · Hooks #4–12 3XL · Freshwater · Pattern by Pat Bennett

Video thumbnail from YouTube.

Rainbow TroutBrown TroutBrook Trout

A weighted chenille stonefly with rubber legs and tails: simple, durable and one of the best big nymphs there is.

▶ Watch it tied on YouTube

Hatch & entomology

Imitates: Large stonefly nymphs (salmonflies, golden stones)

When: All year; especially spring before the stonefly hatch

How to fish it

Fish it deep under an indicator in fast water, often with a small nymph trailed behind.

Materials

  • Hook: 3X-long nymph hook, #4–12
  • Weight: Lead-free wire
  • Thread: Brown or black 6/0
  • Legs and tails: Round rubber legs
  • Body: Variegated chenille

Step by step

  1. Wrap lead-free wire over the middle of the shank and cover it with thread.
  2. Tie in a pair of rubber legs at the bend as the tails, and the chenille.
  3. Wind the chenille forward a third, tie in a set of legs each side; repeat twice more.
  4. Finish the chenille behind the eye, tie in short antennae if you like, and whip finish.
