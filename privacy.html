Fly Bench Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026. Applies to the Fly Bench app on the web (app.flytyingrecipes.org), Android and Windows, and to flytyingrecipes.org.

Fly Bench is a free hobby app run by Chris Roberts in Jacksonville, Florida. This page explains what the app stores, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or advertising cookies.

What we collect

DataWhyWho can see it
Email address and passwordTo sign you in. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.Only you. Never shown to other members.
Display nameShown as the author of recipes you share.Everyone, on recipes you share.
Fly recipes, materials lists, steps, step photos and YouTube links you addTo publish your recipe.Everyone if you choose "Share with everyone"; otherwise only you.
Materials inventory, including barcodes you scan and photos you add of your materialsTo track your tying bench and show which flies you can tie.Only you. Barcode-to-product matches (not your quantities) are shared so other members' scans work.

Photos you send for species ID or receipt readingTo identify the fish or read the receipt.Sent to our AI provider for processing only (see below).

Location is collected only when you add a catch and allow your device to share it. The camera is used only when you scan a barcode or take a photo.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and photo storage (United States).
  • Anthropic (Claude) processes photos you choose to send for species ID or receipt reading. Photos are sent only when you tap the button, are used only to answer that request, and are not used to train AI models under Anthropic's commercial terms.
  • YouTube plays videos that members link in recipes, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube's own privacy policy applies when you play a video.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app's files. Like any web server it briefly logs IP addresses for security.

The 🛒 Buy buttons open a search for that material at the store you pick in Account (Google Shopping, Amazon or eBay). We don't get anything from the store and can't see what you buy there. The app keeps a short list of what you tapped Buy on, only on your device, so it can ask whether to add those items to your bench.

The "Share to iNaturalist" button only opens iNaturalist; nothing is sent unless you upload it there yourself.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your login, profile, recipes (including shared ones and their photos), materials inventory and fish log (including catch photos) right away. Backup copies kept by our hosting provider expire automatically on its normal schedule.

If you can't open the app, request deletion through the contact form using the email address on your account, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Children

Fly Bench is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Each member's private data is protected by database rules so only that member's signed-in account can read it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and note the change in the app. Questions: use the contact form.
